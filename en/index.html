Swimming lessons in La Molina, Lima

ISANAT is a swimming school in La Molina. We teach babies from 6 months, children aged 3 to 15 and adults from 16, in the pool of Colegio Villa Caritas, Urb. Rinconada del Lago. From S/ 280 a month, tax included and with no joining fee.

Programs and ages

Three programs, one pool. Every class is taught by an instructor — there is no unsupervised lane rental.

Aquababy · 6 months to 2 years

Water familiarization for babies from 6 months up to 2 years, with a grown-up in the water. Each session lasts 45 minutes.

Why families in La Molina choose us

Inside La Molina, in Rinconada del Lago

Classes are held in the pool of Colegio Villa Caritas, at Calle Hurón 409. You do not need to cross Lima for your child to learn to swim — or to swim yourself.

From 6 months to adults, in the same pool

If two people of different ages swim in your household, everything is arranged in one conversation and in one place.

A pool rated healthy by MINSA

The pool where we teach is rated a piscina saludable (healthy pool) by Peru's Ministry of Health. Slots run from 6 in the morning to 9 at night, and enrolment is arranged over WhatsApp — no forms, no paperwork.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to speak Spanish?

You are welcome to write to us in English. Our team replies in Spanish, and most families handle it with a translation app without any trouble.

How old does my child need to be?

Aquababy takes babies from 6 months to 2 years, always with a grown-up in the water. From 3 to 15 years old children join the children's program, and from 16 the adult one.

How much does it cost and is there a joining fee?

From S/ 280 a month for one session a week up to S/ 580 for five, tax included. There is no joining fee and no registration charge: you pay only the monthly fee.

Where exactly is the pool?

In Colegio Villa Caritas, Calle Hurón 409, Urb. Rinconada del Lago, La Molina, Lima.

How do I enrol?

Choose a time slot and how often you want to come, message us on WhatsApp at +51 915 236 322 and we confirm availability. We then send you the payment details.

Is there a discount for Villa Caritas or San Pedro families?

Yes. The VCSP community — pupils, former pupils and parents of Colegio Villa Caritas and Colegio San Pedro — gets 40% off the regular prices.

Ready to start?

Tell us the age of the swimmer and which days suit you, and we will confirm availability. We reply in Spanish.
